About CodeOnBaiti

One studio. Three disciplines. Every brand consistent.

CodeOnBaiti Media is a digital and creative studio for businesses, organizations and individuals. We design and build websites and apps, develop brand identities, and manage social media — so our clients can establish a strong presence, reach their audience and grow.
